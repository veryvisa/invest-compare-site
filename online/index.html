在线办理：开户前的三张表，在家就能填好

想好了开哪类账户，就可以把开户前要准备的资料在线交给顾问。从第一张表进去就行，后两张的专属链接会自动发到您的邮箱。

  1. 填「开户基本资料」：写下您的邮箱后提交。
  2. 查邮箱：几秒内收到客户编号，以及另外两张表的专属链接（编号、姓名、邮箱已填好）。
  3. 接着填完另外两张；顾问据此给建议，并在机构的正式申请里为您开户。

提交之后会怎样

每张表提交后，您都会收到回执和修改链接；顾问同时收到通知，看完后联系您，讲清适合哪类账户、在哪家开、费用是多少，再通过机构的正式申请和电子签名开户。在线表只是开户前的准备，不是开户申请本身。

谁能看、怎么改

只有您和顾问本人能看到，资料只提供给为您开户的保险公司或投资机构。资料有变化，用回执里的修改链接改；每年复核时，用邮件里同一条「投资需要与风险评估」链接重新填一份，旧的评估会原样保留。弄丢了邮件，告诉顾问即可重发。

三张表都不收社会保险号、银行账号、证件号码和外国税号；需要时，在机构自己的电子申请或身份核验环节提供。在线版由 Google 表单收集，资料可能存放在加拿大境外；不想用在线版，用 PDF 填好发给顾问即可。

Apply online: three forms before opening an account, done from home

Once you know which account you want, you can send the details your advisor needs online. Start with the first form; private links for the other two arrive in your email automatically.

  1. Fill in "Account opening details" with your email and submit.
  2. Check your email: within seconds you get a client number and private links to the other two forms, with number, name and email already filled in.
  3. Complete the other two; your advisor reviews them, advises you and opens the account through the institution’s formal application.

What happens after you submit

After each form you receive a receipt with an edit link, and your advisor is notified. They then contact you to explain which account fits, where to open it and what it costs, and open it through the institution’s formal application and e-signature. The online forms prepare the account; they are not the application itself.

Who sees it and how to change it

Only you and your advisor can see it, and it goes only to the insurer or investment firm opening your account. If something changes, use the edit link in your receipt; at the yearly review, fill in the same "Investment needs" link again and the earlier assessment is kept as it was. If you lose the email, ask your advisor to resend it.

None of the forms asks for a SIN, bank account, ID number or foreign tax number; when needed, you provide those in the institution’s own e-application or identity check. The online version runs on Google Forms and data may be stored outside Canada; if you prefer, fill in the PDF and send it to your advisor.
